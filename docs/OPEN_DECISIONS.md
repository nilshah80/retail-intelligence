| 83 | Launch-week cold-start comparator eligibility | **DECIDED** | Decision #82's "complete origin-visible weeks" leaves 54 measured `forecast_origin × SeriesKey` pairs (1,404 rows) with one partial prior week and no complete week, so the cold-start gate returns `insufficient_evidence` for every rolling-origin panel containing a series' launch week and no version can ever be accepted. **Option (a) is decided**: when a cold-start row has no complete origin-visible prior week, admit the exposure-normalised `weekly_units_equivalent` of its most recent partial origin-visible week.
